# Office Joke Candidates

1. "My coworker asked if I wanted coffee. I said no thanks, I'm already addicted to the sweet sweet release of quitting time." (168 chars)

2. "The office microwave just died. Turns out three minutes is an eternity when you're reheating yesterday's ambition." (146 chars)

3. "Our company's 'agile' process: we move quickly to the next meeting where we discuss moving quickly." (139 chars)

4. "The boss said 'let's circle back' five hours ago. I think we've circled back to the beginning of time." (147 chars)

5. "My productivity peaked when I discovered that deleting unread emails feels like achieving something." (159 chars)

## Chosen

#2 - The office microwave joke wins because it has a clear setup (microwave death) and punchline about time perception, the twist is unexpected (comparing microwave time to eternity), it rings true for anyone who's reheated lunch, and the wording is tight with no wasted words.
